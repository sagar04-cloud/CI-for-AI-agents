# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in DoneGate a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

- Demonstrate empathy, kindness, and respect for differing viewpoints.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community and users.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `https://github.com/sagar04-cloud/CI-for-AI-agents/issues`.
